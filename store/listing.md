## Full description (max 4000)
```
Tally is the habit tracker for people who are tired of bloated apps. No account.
No ads. No subscription. Nothing leaves your phone. Just open it, tap to check off
your habits, and watch your streaks grow.

Most habit apps want your email, your money, and your data. Tally wants none of it.
It's calm, fast, beautiful — and completely private.

WHY YOU'LL LOVE IT
• One-tap check-ins — building a habit should take a second, not a setup wizard.
• Streaks that motivate — see your current run and your all-time best, with a
  forgiving rule so an unfinished today never breaks your streak.
• Beautiful heatmaps — a GitHub-style calendar shows your consistency at a glance.
• Flexible schedules — track a habit every day, or only on the weekdays you choose.
• Gentle reminders — optional on-device notifications at a time you pick.
• Light & dark themes — matches your system, or set it yourself.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account or sign-up.
• No ads, ever.
• No subscriptions or hidden paywalls.
• No analytics or tracking.
• Works 100% offline — your data stays on your device.

PERFECT FOR
Drinking more water, exercising, reading, meditating, journaling, taking
medication, stretching, studying, quitting a bad habit, or any small daily ritual
that adds up to a better you.

Start your first streak today. It's free, and it always will be.
```
