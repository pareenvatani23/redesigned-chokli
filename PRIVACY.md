# Privacy Policy — Tally

_Last updated: 18 June 2026_

Tally is built to respect your privacy completely.

## The short version

**Tally does not collect, transmit, store off-device, sell, or share any of your
data. Ever.** There is no account, no server, and no analytics.

## What data Tally stores

Tally stores the habits you create and your check-in history **locally on your
device only**, using the operating system's standard on-device storage. This
includes:

- Habit names, icons, colors, and schedules you choose.
- The dates you mark a habit as complete.
- Your app preferences (such as theme).

This information never leaves your phone. We have no ability to access it.

## Data we do **not** collect

- No personal information (no name, email, phone number, or account).
- No location data.
- No advertising identifiers.
- No usage analytics or crash telemetry.
- No contacts, photos, or files.

## Network access

Tally functions entirely offline. The app makes no network requests to us or to
any third party for its core functionality.

## Notifications

If you enable reminders, Tally schedules **local** notifications on your device.
These are generated on-device by the operating system and are not sent through
any external push service.

## Children's privacy

Because Tally collects no data at all, it is safe for users of all ages.

## Deleting your data

You can erase all stored data at any time from **Settings → Erase all data**.
Uninstalling the app also removes all of its data from your device.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published here with a new
"last updated" date.
